Real problem Slide 2 / 7

Why note-taking fails in live classes

In live classes and conference talks, note-taking is an attention trade-off: listen now or Taking notes now.

Cognitive split

You cannot deeply listen and write full notes at the same time.

Speed mismatch

Speakers move fast; your notes lag and lose key context.

English complexity

Accents + technical terms + complex concepts increase processing load.

Current state high attention cost during class, low quality notes after class
Opportunity capture first in realtime, structure second when the lecture ends

This is not only a productivity issue - it is a learning quality issue.

Bridge: "If attention is the bottleneck, the product must capture first and structure later."

Why it matters Slide 9 / 11

Impact

  • Immediate (during class): students keep attention on meaning while notes are captured automatically.
  • Short-term (after class): teachers and learners get cleaner recap material in minutes, not hours.
  • Long-term (knowledge): saved folders + editor tools turn one lecture into reusable study assets.
  • Market fit: multilingual UI (vi, en, id, th, ms) lowers adoption friction across SEA classrooms.

Core impact: reduce note-taking cognitive load now, then increase review quality later.
