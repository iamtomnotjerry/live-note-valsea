# Interview Prep Coach - 2 Minute Demo Script

## Demo setup (before judges)

- Open `/<locale>/interview` (recommend `/en/interview`).
- Internet stable and microphone permission granted.
- Keep one fallback text answer ready in notepad in case microphone fails.

## Target flow (120 seconds)

### 0:00 - 0:15 | Problem framing

Say:
"Vietnamese candidates often fail not because they lack skill, but because they lack structured practice in English and international interview style."

Show:

- Interview setup card (Track, Level, Language).

### 0:15 - 0:30 | Start session

Action:

- Select `Software Engineer`, `Mid`, `English`.
- Click **Start interview**.

Say:
"This coach simulates realistic interview rounds and gives immediate, actionable feedback."

### 0:30 - 1:05 | Answer Question 1 (behavioral)

Action:

- Click **Start recording**.
- Speak ~20-25 seconds (STAR style).
- Click **Stop recording**.
- Click **Analyze this answer**.

Suggested answer sample:
"In my previous project, our release failed due to an API timeout issue. My task was to stabilize deployment quickly. I added timeout retries, optimized one slow query, and coordinated updates with QA every two hours. As a result, we recovered service in the same day and reduced similar incidents by around 40 percent in the next sprint."

Say:
"We process voice to transcript, then run AI clarification, interview notes, action items, sentiment, and rubric scoring."

### 1:05 - 1:35 | Show AI feedback + Korean-style rubric

Show:

- Per-question score card.
- Strengths and improvements.

Say:
"Our rubric is tuned for Korean company expectations: ownership, clear status updates, blocker reporting, and ETA commitment - not just grammar."

### 1:35 - 1:55 | End session and save report

Action:

- Complete second question quickly (or use fallback typed answer).
- Reach session summary.
- Click **Save report**.
- Open saved note from link.

Say:
"Candidates can track progress across practice sessions and prepare targeted improvements before real interviews."

### 1:55 - 2:00 | Close with impact

Say:
"This turns interview preparation from random practice into measurable coaching with immediate next actions."

## Judge Q&A quick answers

- **What if realtime fails?**  
  We support manual text fallback and still run the same AI feedback pipeline.
- **How is this different from a generic chatbot?**  
  It is a full workflow: structured question set, voice capture, rubric-based scoring, and persistent progress reports.
- **Business value?**  
  Better interview readiness, faster candidate screening quality, and repeatable coaching at scale.
